# Vehicle asset provenance

`four-door-utility.glb` is a generic four-door SUV sourced from [3DAssets.dev](https://3dassets.dev/assets/battle-royale-town-and-airfield-suv-four-door-79414f02). The listing identifies the asset as CC0 1.0 Universal and reports that AI was used in its creation. The provider's license and origin statements are retained here as listing claims; they have not been independently verified. The model has no manufacturer branding and is used only as a generic service-vehicle visual, not as a recreation of a real make or model.

The delivered GLB is about 303 kB and contains roughly 22,300 triangles, 30 mesh objects and seven materials. It has four named wheel pivots (`wheel-lf`, `wheel-rf`, `wheel-lr`, `wheel-rr`) and door/wheel animation groups. The game uses the four wheel pivots for steering and spin and does not play the supplied door animations.
